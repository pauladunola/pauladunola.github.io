Biography

From field breeding to digital decision support.

Paul Motunrayo Adunola is a plant breeder and Research Assistant Scientist in the University of Florida Blueberry Breeding & Genomics Lab, where he leads the Phenomics and Digital Breeding Team. His research integrates plant breeding, high-throughput phenotyping, computer vision, robotics, quantitative genetics, genomics, and environmental information to improve selection decisions and cultivar development.

His current research includes robotic and image-based blueberry yield phenotyping, computer vision for plant health, cultivar development for robotic harvesting, multi-domain prediction of blueberry yield, genotype-by-environment analysis, target-environment prediction, and breeder-facing recommendation systems.

Paul earned a Ph.D. in Horticultural Science from the University of Florida. His earlier training in plant breeding and crop science spans France, Finland, Nigeria, and the United States. He has also worked in breeding and quantitative genetics roles with KWS, Corteva Agriscience, the International Institute of Tropical Agriculture, and the University of Florida.

He values interdisciplinary collaboration, mentoring, and research that can move from analysis to practical use in breeding programs. Outside research, he enjoys travel, music, small-canvas acrylic painting, cooking, and pickleball.
